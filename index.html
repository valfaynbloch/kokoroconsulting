心

Kokoro means heart, mind and spirit.

Too often, growing an organisation means exhausting the land or the people who care for it. I believe it doesn't have to. When mind, heart and spirit move together, strategy sustains rather than drains.

We are in a new era of regeneration, where caring for the earth and caring for people are one mission.

How we would work together

Every collaboration grows step by step, and it always starts with a conversation.

A first call

We talk about your mission and see if we are aligned in purpose.

二

A diagnosis

In the first month, I map where you are, what is missing and how we could move forward.

三

On the ground

I come to the land and work alongside your team. You can't understand a project from the outside: living its day-to-day shows what truly serves you.

四

Ongoing support

Support tailored to what your organisation needs: from one day a week, to a couple of days, to simply a call when you need it.
