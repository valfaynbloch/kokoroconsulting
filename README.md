# Personal site: Kokoro Consulting

A place where NGOs can learn how I work, see past work, understand my values,
read my articles, and book a call.

## Audience
NGOs that might hire me as a consultant.

## Phase 1 pages
1. Home: who I am and how I help NGOs
2. About & values: how I work and what I stand for
3. Work / case studies: past projects and the NGOs I've helped
4. Services: what working with me looks like and what to expect
5. Articles: my writing
6. Book a call: visitors pick a time synced to Google Calendar

## Requirements
- Edit content in a visual editor in the browser (no code needed to publish)
- Two languages: English + Spanish. Default is English with an ES switch (assumed; confirm)
- Booking synced with Google Calendar
- Open source and self-hostable throughout
- Brand name: Kokoro Consulting. Domain not registered yet
- Launch content: about 3 case studies; articles still being written.
  Build with placeholder content first and swap in the real content as it's ready

## Design references
- https://www.slrconsulting.com/apac/careers/ (sustainability consultancy). Patterns:
  a big purpose-driven hero headline, value cards, testimonial cards with headshots,
  a stats row, CTAs repeated at the top/middle/bottom, and a warm, direct "we/you" tone.
- https://www.slrconsulting.com/apac/ (homepage). Patterns: a short mission headline,
  3 "pillar" cards for service areas, a bold manifesto statement, a "Latest thinking"
  row of article cards (date + reading time), a project grid with tags (client, region,
  sector), a newsletter sign-up in the footer, and real-world photography.
- She likes all of SLR: straightforward, beautiful and photo-rich, with a direct, purpose-driven tone. This is the image to project.
