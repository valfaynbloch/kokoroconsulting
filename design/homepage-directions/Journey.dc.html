What the land taught me

You can't understand something only from the concept of it. You have to experience it.

Working side by side with the people who run regenerative projects has been the most gratifying experience of my life, and my biggest gift. That is why, when I start working with a project, I come to the land.

Living its day-to-day shows me what is truly good for the founders and how they already work. That is invaluable. Afterwards, we can keep building systems and tools together from a distance.

Today I look for regenerative projects run with integrity and coherence, by people who have their hands in the soil and are not just talking about what can be done, but doing it.
